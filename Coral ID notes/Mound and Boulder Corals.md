# Obricella Corals
## Orbicella Faveolata - OFAV
- Small round polyps with exsert walls in mounds with thick plates
  - Surfaces smooth, ridged or with bumps aligned in vertical rows![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.06.27 PM.png|577]]![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.09.45 PM.png]]
- Colouration ranges from green, brown, grey
  - May fluoresce 
- Can grow 4-5m tall and 12-15ft wide
## Obricella Annularis - OANN
- Small, round, exsert polyps are alive at top of column![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.18.02 PM.png]]
  - Columns may topple and scatter during storms if bases are narrowed by bioerosion![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.17.49 PM.png]]
- Thick plates at colony sides or bases
- Light brown or yellow-brown in colour
- 3-4m tall and 9-12ft wide - large colonies
## Orbicella Franksi - OFRA
- Irregular bumps with large, exsert polyps![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.18.46 PM.png]]
  - polyps are pale or lack zooxanthellae
  - Large polyps along colony margin
  - Irregularly shaped mounds in shallow/high light![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.20.35 PM.png]]
  - Thick, lumpy plates in deep/low light![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.20.42 PM.png]]
- 3-4m tall and 9-12ft wide
### OFAV Vs OANN
- OANN subdivides to form columns with basal plates under low light conditions
- Live polyps on well illuminated column tops lack a skirt like edge
- Lighter tissue colour
### OFAV and OFRA
- Have bumps on large mounds, crusts or thick plates
### OFAV Vs OFRA
- OFRA has larger bumps 
  - Polyps on bumps are even bigger, irregularly shaped 
  - Often lack zooxanthellae
- OFRA is more aggressive as a spatial competitor 
### OFRA and OANN
- Can form large columns or thick plates
### OFRA Vs OANN
- OFRA has enlarged polyps in irregular bumps 
  - Can lack zooxanthellae 
- Polyps along growing margins are enlarged in OFRA
- OFRA is more aggressive as a spatial competitor 

# Corals to Ignore - SBOU and SHYA
## Solenastrea Bournoni - SBOU
- Polyps are separated by a smooth inter-polyp surface
- Cream to light brown in colour
  - Polyps are slightly darker than the inter-polyp spaces![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.30.15 PM.png|449]]
- Smooth or irregularly shaped mounds![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.30.29 PM.png]]
- 50cm tall and 20in wide
## Solenastrea Hyades - SHYA
- Light coloured
- Polyps with distinct walls
- Large lobes above an encrusting base              ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.32.35 PM.png|367]]
- 60cm tall and 2ft wide
# Montastraea Cavernosa - MCAV
- Large round, exsert polyps                                                           ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.35.38 PM.png|351]]![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.35.46 PM.png|456]]
- Mounds or columns in shallow/high light                         ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.36.08 PM.png|399]]
- Flattened massive plates or crusts in deep/low light![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.36.34 PM.png|427]]
- Brown, yellow-brown, green or fray in colour
  - Sometimes fluorescent colours 
- 3m tall and 9ft wide
# Dichocoenia Stokesi - DSTO
- Very exsert, round, elliptical, elongate or T-shaped polyps![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.38.29 PM.png]]
- Cream, yellow or brown in colour
- Mounds or irregular shapes                                       ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.38.12 PM.png|326]]
- 50 cm tall and 18in wide
### DSTO Vs MCAV 
- DSTO polyps are much more variable in shape
- Septa on vertical walls are more distinct in DSTO
- Fuller grown colonies of DSTO are smaller
- DSTO has lighter colours
# Favia Fragum - FFRA
- Somewhat exsert, round-elongated polyps                        ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.54.35 PM.png|397]]
- Septa have small teeth
- Pale yellow to brown
- 10cm tall to 4in wide - small mounds
### FFRA and DSTO
- Exsert, round-elongated polyps, some are Y-shaped 
- Similar colours
### FFRA Vs DSTO
- Polyp walls protrude far less in FFRA
- Polyps are less separate in FFRA
- FFRA septa with teeth on summits and inner sides
- FFRA fully grown colonies are smaller
# Siderastrea siderea - SSID
- Small, sunken polyps with very thin septa                   ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 1.59.44 PM.png|473]]
- Uniform colours - grey, yellow-brown to brown
  - Some pale or bleached                                 ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 1.59.53 PM.png|406]]
  - Colonies are fluorescent
- Rounded mounds
- 2m tall and 6ft wide
# Siderastrea Radians - SRAD
- Suncken polyps with thick septa
- Pale polyp walls with dark centers                    ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 2.01.13 PM.png|423]]
- Crusts, low mounds or unattached nodules
- 30 cm tall and 12in wide
### SRAD Vs SSID
- SRAD has fewer and thicker septa
- SRAD has pinched polyps, some elongate and with dark centers
- SRAD fully grown colonies are smaller and flatter
# Stephanocoenia Intersepta - SINT
- Round sunken polyps with thick septa                   ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 2.23.29 PM.png|496]]
- Brown colour is most intense in polyp centers
  - Appear to blush when polyps contract
- Thick crusts, irregular mounds
- 1m tall and 3ft wide
### SINT and SBOU
- Small, round polyps with light brownish colours that are most intense in polyp center
### SINT Vs SBOU
- SINT has sunken polyps
- SINT forms rusts and low mounds have relatively smooth surfaces without large bumps
### SINT and SRAD
- SINT has sunken polyps with dark centers
### SINT Vs SRAD
- SINT blushes when tissues contract
- SINT doesn't have pinched polyps
- SINT has larger crusts and mounds when fully grown
# Porites Asteroides - PAST
- Tall thin polyps look fuzzy when expanded                         ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 2.25.33 PM.png|497]]
- Lumpy mounds or plates
  - Shallow corals are usually lumpy mounds and yellow, yellow-green or olive![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 2.26.09 PM.png|475]]
  - Deep corals are usually thick crusts, lumpy or flattened plates and grey or brown![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 2.26.44 PM.png|360]]
- Unusual shape or colour combinations can occur even within a single coral
- 1m tall and 3ft wide
