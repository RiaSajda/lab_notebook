# Obricella Corals
## Orbicella Faveolata - OFAV
- Small round polyps with exsert walls in mounds with thick plates
  - Surfaces smooth, ridged or with bumps aligned in vertical rows![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.06.27 PM.png|577]]![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.09.45 PM.png]]
- Colouration ranges from green, brown, grey
  - May fluoresce 
- Can grow 4-5m tall and 12-15ft wide
## Obricella Annularis - OANN
- Small, round, exsert polyps are alive at top of column![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.18.02 PM.png|428]]
  - Columns may topple and scatter during storms if bases are narrowed by bioerosion![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.17.49 PM.png|375]]
- Thick plates at colony sides or bases
- Light brown or yellow-brown in colour
- 3-4m tall and 9-12ft wide - large colonies
## Orbicella Franksi - OFRA
- Irregular bumps with large, exsert polyps![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.18.46 PM.png]]
  - polyps are pale or lack zooxanthellae
  - Large polyps along colony margin
  - Irregularly shaped mounds in shallow/high light![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.20.35 PM.png]]
  - Thick, lumpy plates in deep/low light![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.20.42 PM.png]]
- 3-4m tall and 9-12ft wide
### OFAV Vs OANN
- OANN subdivides to form columns with basal plates under low light conditions
- Live polyps on well illuminated column tops lack a skirt like edge
- Lighter tissue colour
### OFAV and OFRA
- Have bumps on large mounds, crusts or thick plates
### OFAV Vs OFRA
- OFRA has larger bumps 
  - Polyps on bumps are even bigger, irregularly shaped 
  - Often lack zooxanthellae
- OFRA is more aggressive as a spatial competitor 
### OFRA and OANN
- Can form large columns or thick plates
### OFRA Vs OANN
- OFRA has enlarged polyps in irregular bumps 
  - Can lack zooxanthellae 
- Polyps along growing margins are enlarged in OFRA
- OFRA is more aggressive as a spatial competitor

# Montastraea Cavernosa - MCAV
- Large round, exsert polyps                                                           ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.35.38 PM.png|351]]
- Mounds or columns in shallow/high light                         ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.36.08 PM.png|399]]
- Flattened massive plates or crusts in deep/low light![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 12.36.34 PM.png|427]]
- Brown, yellow-brown, green or fray in colour
  - Sometimes fluorescent colours 
- 3m tall and 9ft wide
# Diploria Labyrinthiformis - DLAB
- Prominent groove along the top of the ridges 
  - Can be deeper or wider than the valleys![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 4.34.08 PM.png]]
- Tan to brown colours
  - Valleys may fluoresce 
  - 1m tall and 3ft wide
# Pseudodiploria Stigosa - PSTR
- Groove along ridge tops is very narrow and often indistinct
- Yellow-brown, green, brown or bluish grey
  - Valleys may be lighter or fluoresce 
- Mounds, thick crusts or massive plates
- 2m tall and 6ft wide
- Forms mounds or thick crusts in shallow/high light![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 4.49.53 PM.png|448]]
- Forms massive plates in deep/low light                    ![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 4.50.05 PM.png|327]]
