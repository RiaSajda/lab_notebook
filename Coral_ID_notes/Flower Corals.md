# Colpophyllia Natans - CNAT
- Narrow grooves along the tops, and midway down the sides of the ridges![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 4.23.23 PM.png]]
- Ridges have narrow septa
  - Narrow septa connect polyp mouths
- Tissue may have 2 colours or two shades of one colour
  - May have stripes on ridges
  - Valleys may be fluorescent 
- Mounds form in shallow/high light 
- Thick plates form in deep/low light
- 3m tall and 10ft wide

# IGNORE: Manicina Areolata - MARE
- Distinct grooves in ridge tops
  - Vertical ridge sides
- Free-living or attached
- Yellow-brown, grey or green
  - May have lighter patches in ridge tops
- 20cm tall and 8in wide

### MARE Vs CNAT
- MARE has wider grooves in the ridges
- Polyp mouths not connected by septa in MARE
- MARE colonies are smaller when fully grown
# Diploria Labyrinthiformis - DLAB
- Prominent groove along the top of the ridges 
  - Can be deeper or wider than the valleys![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 4.34.08 PM.png]]
- Tan to brown colours
  - Valleys may fluoresce 
  - 1m tall and 3ft wide
### DLAB Vs MARE
- DLAB has narrower valleys
- DLAB has grooves in all ridges
  - Can be much deeper and wider
- DLAB tentacles often partially expanded by day
- DLAB colonies are larger when fully grown
# Pseudodiploria Stigosa - PSTR
- Groove along ridge tops is very narrow and often indistinct
- Yellow-brown, green, brown or bluish grey
  - Valleys may be lighter or fluoresce 
- Mounds, thick crusts or massive plates
- 2m tall and 6ft wide
- Forms mounds or thick crusts in shallow/high light![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 4.49.53 PM.png]]
- Forms massive plates in deep/low light![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 4.50.05 PM.png]]
## PSTR Vs DLAB Vs CNAT Vs MARE
- PSTR grooves along ridge tops are very narrow and often indistinct 
- PSTR has narrower valleys![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 4.51.50 PM.png]]
# Pseudodiploria Clivosa - PCLI
- Narrow ridges lack grooves
- Shallow, narrow valleys![[Screenshot 2026-05-22 at 4.54.51 PM.png]]
- Yellow-brown, green, brown or bluish or grey
  - Valleys may be lighter than ridges or a different colour
  - Valleys may be fluoresce 
- Flattened or irregular, lumpy crusts
- 1m tall and 3ft wide
- Shallow depth range
## PCLI Vs CNAT
- PCLI has smaller ridges which lack grooves
- PCLI has smaller valleys
- PCLI septa are thinner and more numerous
- PCLI has flat or lumpy crusts
- PCLI is only found in shallow depths
# Meandrina species
## Meandrina Meandrites - MMEA
- Wide ridges with large, thick septa![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 9.38.13 AM.png]]
- Deep, narrow valleys
- Pale yellow to dark orange or dark brown
- Thick plates or crusts, mounds or short columns![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 9.39.15 AM.png]]
- 1m tall and 3ft wide
- Very aggressive towards most other corals
### MMEA Vs MARE
- MMEA has thick ridges with large, thick septa
- MMEA has deep narrow valleys in which narrow septa connect polyp mouths![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 9.45.15 AM.png]]
## IGNORE: Meandrina Jacksoni - MJAC
- Low, narrow ridges with short, thick widely spaced septa![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 9.47.39 AM.png]]
- Wide, shallow valleys
- White tentacles (usually conspicuous)
- Usually pale cream or pale yellow
- Mounds, thick plates or crusts, short columns or irregular shapes
- 1m tall and 3ft wide
### MJAC Vs MMEA
- MJAC has shallower, wider valleys
- MJAC has lower, narrower ridges, with lower and more widely spaced septa
- Polyps more likely to be visible by day
### MJAC Vs PCLI
- MJAC has wider valleys
- MJAC has wider ridges with larger septa
- MJAC polyps more likely to be visible by day
![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 9.52.22 AM.png|551]]

# Dendrogyra Cylindrus - DCYL
- Tall columns above a massive base
- Polyps usually expanded by day
- Tan, yellow-brown to dark brown
- 3m tall and 10ft wide
## DCYL Vs Meandrina species
- DCYL Polyps are usually fully expanded by day
- DCYL has very deep, narrow valleys
- Ridges are usually wide and flat on top in DCYL
- DCYL colonies are much larger when fully grown
![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 9.58.46 AM.png]]
# Mycetophylia species- MYCE
- Polyp mouths connected by septa with distinct teeth - Applicable to all species![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 10.05.17 AM.png]]
## Mycetophyllia ferox - MFER
- Thick crusts or plates with narrow ridges that fork, subdividing some of the narrow valleys
- Raised septal margins around polyp mouths
- Dull of fluorescent colours
- 60cm tall and 2ft wide
- Aggressive towards may other corals ![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 10.09.03 AM.png]]
## Mycetophyllia Aliciae - MALI
- Thick crusts or plates
- Some wide valleys
  - Ridges radiate away away from the colony center
- Brown, green, yellow-brown or yellow-green
  -  May have white or pale green spots of lines on ridges and on the raised septal margins around the mouths
  - 75cm tall and 30in wide
  ![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 10.16.17 AM.png]]
### MALI and MFER
- Both have thick crusts and plates
- Raised septal margins around polyp mouths
### MALI Vs MFER
- MALI has taller ridges that rarely intersect and wider valleys
  - All radiating away from colony center
- MALI has brighter colours 
![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 10.18.44 AM.png]]
## Mycetophyllia Lamarckiana Complex - MLAM
- Forms mounds or inverted cones![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 10.20.49 AM.png]]
- Septal margins aren't raised around polyp mouths
- Brown, yellow-brown, grey or green
  - Ridges may be pale
- 40cm tall and 16 in wide
### MLAM Vs MALI Vs MFER
- MLAM has mounds or inverted cones
  - Not thick crusts or plates
- MLAM septal margins not raised around polyp mouths
- MLAM colonies are smaller when fully grown
# Isophyllia species 
## Isophyllia sinuosa - ISIN
- Fleshy polyps
- Larger ridges with distinct septal teeth
- Short, deep valleys![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 10.29.49 AM.png]]
- Grey, green, yellow or brown
  - May have several colours or fluoresce 
- 20cm tall and 8in wide
  - Small mounds
### ISIN and MLAM
- Both form mounds with large septal teeth
### ISIN Vs MLAM
- ISIN has fleshier polyps
- ISIN polyps mouth aren't linked by septa
## Isophyllia Rigida - IRIG
- Distinct septal teeth
- Very short, irregularly shaped valleys with 1-3 polyp mouths
- Ridge tissue often tan or brown
  - Valleys pale or white
- 20cm tall and 8in wide
-  Small mounds
### IRIG and ISIN
- IRIG has very fleshy polyps
- IRIG has large septal teeth
- IRIG has thin groove along ridge tops
### IRIG Vs ISIN
- IRIG has shorter valleys 
- IRIG has different colour patterns
![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 10.41.25 AM.png]]
# Scolymia species - SCOL
- solitary polyps
  - Occasionally more than one mouth
- Large septal teeth
## Scolymia Cubensis - SCUB
- Teeth may be visible beneath tissues![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 10.46.14 AM.png]]
- Brown, green or grey
  - Maybe have lighter stripes of streaks
  - May be fluoresce 
## Scolymia Lacera - SLAC
- Very fleshy polyps with "warts" over the very large septal teeth![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 10.46.29 AM.png|388]]
- Pale or opaque, grey, green, blue-green or brown
  - May be lighter over the septa
- 15cm tall and 6in wide
- Vey aggressive spatial competitor 
### SLAC Vs SCUB
- SLAC has larger, more fleshy polyps
- SLAC has duller colors
  SLAC is a more aggressive spatial competitor ![[Screenshot 2026-05-26 at 10.47.45 AM.png]]
# Mussa Angulosa - MANG
- Large, fleshy polyps are alive only at top of "stalks"
- Large septal teeth
- Opaque greys, greens, yellow-browns often mottled
  - Sometimes fluoresce pinks or reds
- 1m tall and 3ft wide
- Very aggressive spatial competitor
# Eusmilia Fastigiata - EFAS
- Round to ovoid or elongate, stalked polyps only alive at tips
- Smooth septa visible through tissues 
- Yellow-brown, brown or grey
- 75cm tall and 30in wide
## EFAS and MANG
- Stalked polyps only alive at tips 
## EFSA Vs MANG
- Polyps are smaller and less fleshy in EFAS
- EFAS colours are uniformly pale
- Septa lack teeth
